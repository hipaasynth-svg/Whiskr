Official rules

Whiskr Cat of the Month Contest

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A purchase does not improve your chances of winning. Void where prohibited by law.

Sponsor

This contest is operated by Whiskr (whiskr.lol), a HipAAsynth LLC brand. Contact: see the email address in any contest email, or the address in this site's footer once published.

Eligibility

  • Open to legal residents of the countries this site ships to, who are 18 years of age or older at the time of entry.
  • You must own the submitted photo, or have the owner's permission to submit and license it, and confirm this via the entry form's consent checkbox.
  • One entry per cat per contest round. Whiskr may remove duplicate entries at its discretion.
  • Employees of Whiskr/HipAAsynth LLC and their immediate household members are not eligible to enter.

How to enter

Submit one photo of your cat and a valid email address through the entry form on the homepage. Entry is free — no purchase, payment, or donation is required or accepted as a condition of entry.

Contest period

Entries and voting are open continuously. Each contest round runs for a fixed period (shown on the entry form and voting page); when a round closes, the next opens immediately. There is no gap where entry is unavailable.

Cat of the Year

Once per year, at a time of Whiskr's choosing, a separate Cat of the Year vote opens among that year's monthly Cat of the Month winners at whiskr.lol/year-award.html, announced there and on the homepage while it's open. No entry or nomination is needed — every eligible cat is automatically included as a finalist. Voting is one ballot per person for the entire award (not once per finalist, and not repeatable), free, no purchase necessary. The finalist with the most votes when this vote closes is Cat of the Year.

How voting works

  • Anyone can vote for any entered cat, once per cat, for free, at whiskr.lol/vote.html.
  • Vote totals are not displayed publicly while a round is open. This is deliberate — it keeps the count honest and prevents early leaders from snowballing purely because they're visible, not because they have more real support.
  • One vote per person per cat is enforced by a combination of a browser identity and connection-level rate limits. This is a good-faith technical control, not a perfect one — see Disqualification below for what happens when it's circumvented anyway.

How winners are determined

When a round closes, every non-disqualified entry is ranked by total vote count, highest to lowest. Ties are broken in favor of whichever entry was submitted earliest. The top entries (the number is stated on the entry form and voting page for that round) become that round's featured cats and appear in that round's calendar; the single highest vote-getter is "Cat of the Month" and appears on the calendar cover.

Every entrant — winner or not — is notified of their final placement (e.g., "placed #47 of 212") once the round closes.

Prizes

Monthly (Cat of the Month): every top-ranked entry is included in that round's printed calendar (cover or interior page), which the entrant and others may purchase. The single highest vote-getter each round is "Cat of the Month" and appears on that round's calendar cover. No cash prize, and no physical prize beyond calendar inclusion, is offered at the monthly level.

Annual (Cat of the Year): once per year, Whiskr opens a separate public vote among that year's monthly Cat of the Month winners. The single highest vote-getter in that vote is "Cat of the Year" and receives one (1) physical, one-of-a-kind wooden sculpture of their own cat, handmade by artist Cody Carlson, at no cost to the winner. This prize's value is capped under $5,000. No cash prize is offered, and no cash-equivalent substitution is available.

Winning any prize does not obligate the winner, or anyone else, to purchase anything, and no purchase improves anyone's odds of winning at either the monthly or annual level.

Disqualification

Whiskr may disqualify any entry, at its sole discretion, for any of the following, without notice:

  • Votes that appear to be purchased, bot-generated, or otherwise artificially inflated.
  • A submitted photo the entrant doesn't own or hold a license to use.
  • Offensive, inappropriate, or unrelated (non-cat) image content.
  • Any attempt to manipulate the entry or voting system outside normal use.

A disqualified entry's existing votes are not deleted (in case a dispute needs to be reviewed) but no longer count toward ranking, and the entry can no longer receive votes.

Photo rights

By submitting a photo, you confirm you own it (or have the rights holder's permission) and grant Whiskr a license to display it publicly on this site and print and sell it as part of calendar and print products, consistent with the consent you affirm on the entry form.

General conditions

Whiskr is not responsible for technical failures of any kind, including lost, delayed, or garbled entries or votes, or for any injury or damage to any device related to or resulting from participation. Whiskr reserves the right to cancel, suspend, or modify the contest, or these rules, if fraud, technical failure, or any factor beyond its control impairs the integrity of the contest, with notice posted on this page.

Questions

Contact Whiskr using the email address shown in any contest-related email you've received, or check the site footer for current contact details.
