Official Whiskr "Cat of the Month" Contest Rules

Last updated: see the date on your entry confirmation email. Entry is free — nothing sold on this site is required to enter or to win.

1. Sponsor

This contest is sponsored by Whiskr (operated by [legal entity name — confirm with the owner]), referred to here as "Sponsor," "we," or "us."

2. Eligibility

Open to anyone who can submit a photo they own the rights to and a valid email address, subject to Sponsor's shipping limitations (see Section 6). Void where prohibited by law. Sponsor's employees/family and anyone involved in judging are not eligible for the bi-monthly grand prize.

3. How to enter

Submit one photo of your cat and your email address through the entry form at whiskr.lol. By entering, you confirm you own the rights to the photo and grant Whiskr a license to print, display, and (if your cat is chosen for the grand prize — see Section 5) paint from it.

4. How batches are judged ("Cat of the Month")

  1. Entries seal into a batch once either 40 photos have been submitted, or 3 weeks have passed since the first entry in that batch — whichever happens first. This means every entrant gets a result within about 3 weeks of entering, regardless of how full the batch is.
  2. A human judge (the Sponsor) reviews every photo in the sealed batch and personally selects one "cover cat" — there is no public voting and no random selection except as a safety-net fallback if the judge misses the judging deadline, in which case one entrant from that batch is chosen at random.
  3. Every entrant in the batch is notified by email once judging closes, whether or not their cat was chosen as the cover cat.
  4. Being chosen as a batch's cover cat carries no cash value and is not itself a prize with monetary value — it's a featured placement, and that batch's cover cat becomes eligible for the grand prize described below. The calendar featuring the batch (cover cat included) is an optional item anyone can purchase; nothing is given away for free at this stage.

5. The bi-monthly grand prize

  1. Prize: one original, one-of-a-kind 11x16 acrylic-on-canvas painting, hand-painted by the Sponsor's artist, of the winning cat. Approximate retail value: $[owner to confirm — materials + time]. No cash or other substitution will be made, except that Sponsor reserves the right to substitute a prize of comparable or greater value if circumstances beyond its control require it.
  2. Eligibility pool: every batch's cover cat (see Section 4) automatically becomes eligible the moment that batch is judged.
  3. Selection: approximately every 2 weeks, the Sponsor personally selects one eligible cover cat to receive the grand prize, at Sponsor's sole discretion. This is a judged choice, not a random drawing, and not based on any purchase.
  4. Odds of winning: depend on the number of cover cats eligible at the time of selection.
  5. Notification and claim: the winner is notified by email with a private link to confirm a shipping address. The painting is of the photo you entered and won with by default — the link also lets you send a different reference photo if you'd prefer.
  6. Fulfillment: your painting will be mailed within 2 weeks of your shipping address being confirmed. We'll email you once it ships.

7. General conditions

  • By entering, you agree these rules are final and Sponsor's decisions on judging are final.
  • Sponsor may disqualify any entry it reasonably believes violates these rules, was not submitted by the person claiming to own the photo, or otherwise appears fraudulent or abusive.
  • Sponsor may amend these rules, or pause or end the contest, at any time; changes apply prospectively and won't affect a grand prize already awarded.
  • Contest is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with any email provider or social platform.
  • Entrant information is used only to administer this contest and for Whiskr communications you can unsubscribe from at any time — see our emails for the unsubscribe link.
