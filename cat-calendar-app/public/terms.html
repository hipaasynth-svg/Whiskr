What Whiskr is

A free monthly cat photo contest decided by real public vote — no purchase necessary to enter or win — where each round's top vote-getter gets a one-of-a-kind original painting of their cat, hand-painted by artist Cody Carlson. Separately, and unrelated to the contest, anyone can order a custom print of their own cat or dog photo (mugs, posters, canvas prints, phone cases, totes, pillows, magnets), made to order and shipped through our print partner.

No accounts, just email

Whiskr doesn't have user accounts or passwords. Entries, votes, and orders are tied to your email address, and links we send you (your status page, your order, a review request) carry a signed token instead of a login — don't share those links with anyone you don't want acting on your behalf.
