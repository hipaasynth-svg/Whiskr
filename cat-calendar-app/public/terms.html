Cat of the Month: official rules

No purchase is necessary to enter or win. Buying a calendar or any other product does not improve your chances.

  • Who can enter: legal residents of the United States who are 18 or older. Void where prohibited.
  • How to enter: submit one photo of a cat you own, with your email address, using the form on our homepage.
  • Batches: entries are grouped in the order received into batches of 12. A batch closes when its 12th entry arrives.
  • Judging: Whiskr staff choose one cover cat from each batch, based on photo quality, charm, and how well it would work as a calendar cover. Judging usually finishes within 21 days of a batch closing. If judging isn't finished by then, the cover cat is chosen at random from that batch.
  • What the winner gets: their cat is featured as the cover of that batch's calendar and named Cat of the Month on our website. There is no cash prize and the recognition has no cash value.
  • All 12 cats in a batch appear in that batch's calendar.
  • Results: every entrant in a batch is emailed when judging closes.

License for contest photos: by entering, you grant Whiskr a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to display your photo and your cat's name on our website and in promotion of the contest, and to print and sell it as part of your batch's calendar. You keep ownership of your photo.

We may remove any entry that isn't a cat, isn't the entrant's own photo, or is inappropriate. The contest is run by Whiskr and is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by any social network.
