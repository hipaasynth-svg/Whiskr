Shipping & returns

Shipping, production time & returns

Last updated September 2026.

This page covers two different things Whiskr sells, and the terms are not the same for both:

  • Custom prints (mugs, posters, canvas prints, phone cases, totes, pillows, magnets) — printed to order through our print partner. Everything from here down to “Cancellations & changes” applies to these.
  • Original acrylic commissions — painted by hand, one of a kind, paid in two parts. These have their own terms: see Original commissions below.

Neither covers the contest's painting prize — that's not a checkout item and isn't bought, so its timeline is handled directly by email with the winner.

Made to order (prints)

Nothing in our print shop is pre-made or kept in stock — every order is printed specifically for you after you place it, through our print partner. Please allow real production time before your order ships, in addition to shipping time to your address.

Where we ship

We ship to the countries offered at checkout. If your country isn't listed there, we don't currently ship to it.

Shipping cost & tax

Shipping and any applicable sales tax are calculated and shown at checkout before you pay — the total you're charged is the total shown.

Damaged, defective, or wrong items (prints)

If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 14 days of delivery with a photo of the issue and we'll replace it or refund it at no cost to you.

Buyer's remorse & changed your mind (prints)

Because every item is printed specifically with your own photo, we can't resell a returned one to anyone else — orders are final sale once production has started, except for the defective/wrong-item case above. Cancel or correct an order as soon as possible after placing it (see below); once it's in production, we can no longer change or cancel it.

Cancellations & changes (prints)

Email us right away if you need to cancel or change an order — we'll do our best if production hasn't started yet, but can't guarantee it once it has.

Original commissions

An original acrylic commission works differently from a print, because it is a one-of-a-kind object made by hand rather than a file sent to a printer.

Two payments. A 40% deposit books your slot. The balance is charged separately, before the painting ships — never automatically. When the painting is finished we email you a photo of it along with a payment link, so you see the finished work before you pay the rest.

How long it takes. Two to four weeks from the day your deposit clears, plus shipping time. If you added rush, under ten days. If anything is going to run past that, we will email you before the deadline passes, not after.

Cancelling, and what happens to the deposit. Email us as soon as you can.

  • If painting hasn't started yet, your deposit is refunded in full.
  • Once painting has started, the deposit is not refundable — it covers materials already bought and studio time already reserved and spent. You will not be charged the balance, and the painting stays with the studio.
  • If you see the finished painting and decide not to go ahead, that is the same case: no balance is charged, the painting isn't shipped, and the deposit is kept.
  • If we cancel for any reason, or can't complete your painting, you get a full refund including the deposit.

If the reference photo doesn't work. Cody will email you and ask for a better one. If no usable photo exists, we refund the deposit in full rather than paint something neither of us is happy with.

Damage in transit. Originals are packed and shipped with care. If yours arrives damaged, contact us within 14 days of delivery with photos. Because the painting is one of a kind we can't send a replacement — we will repair it where that is genuinely possible, or refund you.

What you're buying. You are buying the physical painting. Cody keeps copyright in the artwork itself, and may show photographs of it as an example of his work. You keep ownership of the reference photo you sent.

Order status

You'll get an email confirmation when you order and another when it ships. If you have a question about an order, email us using the address on your order confirmation.
