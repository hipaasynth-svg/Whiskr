Shipping & returns

Last updated September 28, 2026

Made to order

Every Whiskr product is printed just for you, after you order. Our print partner, Printful, produces and ships it directly to you.

  • Production: usually 2 to 7 business days.
  • Shipping: usually 3 to 8 business days after that, within the United States.
  • These are typical times, not guarantees. Carrier delays and holidays can add a few days.

We currently ship to addresses in the United States only.

Contest calendars

Calendars are printed after a contest batch has been judged and you place your order. They ship flat.

Damaged, defective, or misprinted items

If your order arrives damaged or misprinted, or is the wrong item, contact us within 30 days of delivery with your order email and a photo of the problem. We will reprint it or refund you, whichever you prefer. You do not need to send the item back.

Returns for other reasons

Because each item is made from your own photo, we cannot accept returns or exchanges if you change your mind or order the wrong size. Please double-check your product and photo before you pay.

Photo quality

We print the photo you upload as-is. A blurry, dark, or very small photo will print that way too. That is not a defect, so upload the sharpest photo you have.

Address problems

Please check your shipping address at checkout. If a package is returned because of an incorrect address, we can reship it once you cover the new shipping cost.
