Privacy policy

How Whiskr handles your data

Last updated September 2026.

This page covers the free cat photo contest, voting, custom print orders, and reviews on whiskr.lol. It doesn't repeat the official contest rules — see Official contest rules for eligibility, photo licensing, and how winners are picked.

What we collect

  • Entering the contest: your email address, your cat's name, the photo you submit, and your confirmation that you own the photo or have permission to submit it.
  • Voting: a small identity cookie (whiskr_voter) so the same browser can't vote for the same cat twice, and a one-way, salted hash of your IP address — never your raw IP itself — used only to enforce daily vote limits and catch obvious abuse.
  • Ordering a print: your email, the photo you upload, your pet's species and name, your shipping address, and payment details. Your card number never touches our servers — Stripe handles payment directly and we only ever see that a charge succeeded.
  • Leaving a review: your review text and an optional display name, tied to the specific order it's about.
  • If you arrive from an ad: an optional cookie (whiskr_utm_campaign) recording which campaign link you followed, so we can tell which ads are working. It doesn't contain anything personally identifying, just a campaign label.

What we use it for

Running the contest (picking and notifying the winner), fulfilling print orders (sending your photo and shipping address to our printing partner), sending the emails you'd expect (entry confirmation, your vote result, order status, a review request), preventing fraud and abuse, and — if your cat wins — having artist Cody Carlson paint your cat's portrait from the winning photo.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing. The businesses below process it on our behalf, only for the reasons listed:

  • Stripe — processes payment for print orders. Handles your card details directly; we never see or store them.
  • Printful — our print-on-demand partner. Receives your photo, quantity, and shipping address to print and ship your order.
  • Zoho Mail — sends the transactional emails this site relies on (entry confirmation, contest results, order updates).
  • Vercel — hosts the site, stores uploaded photos, and provides basic, aggregate visit analytics.

Cookies

Just the two functional cookies described above — no third-party ad-tracking pixels, and no cookie banner selling your browsing history to anyone. Vercel's analytics is aggregate and doesn't use a tracking cookie to follow you across other sites.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

Entries, orders, and photos are kept as part of our normal operating and contest records. There's no fully automated self-service deletion tool yet — email us (see Contact below) and we'll delete what we hold digitally. The one thing we can't undo is a photo already used in a shipped print or an already-painted portrait; we can still remove it from our own systems going forward.

Your rights

Contact us anytime to see what we have on file, correct it, or ask us to delete it. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link — transactional emails you'd expect from an order you placed or a contest you entered aren't marketing and can't be turned off the same way, but you're always welcome to ask us to delete your data outright.

We're a small, US-based business and don't currently run a formal GDPR/CCPA compliance program, but we'll honor a data access, correction, or deletion request from anyone, anywhere, the same way.

Children

Whiskr isn't directed at children. Contest entry requires being 18 or older — see the official rules.

Changes to this policy

If anything material changes about what we collect or how we use it, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
