Privacy policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you order or enter the contest.
  • The photo you upload and your pet's name, if you give one.
  • Your name and shipping address, collected at checkout so we can ship your order.
  • Payment details, handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Reviews you choose to leave, and the display name you give with them.

How we use it

  • To print, ship, and support your order.
  • To run the contest: judging, telling you the result, and producing the batch calendar.
  • To email you about your order, your contest entry, and a single request to review your order. Emails with an offer include a one-click unsubscribe link.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for other companies' advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to run Whiskr, and only for that purpose:

  • Stripe: payments and sales tax.
  • Printful: printing and shipping (receives your photo, name, address, and email).
  • Vercel: website hosting and photo storage.
  • Zoho: sending our emails.
  • Google Fonts: loads the fonts on this site, which shares your IP address with Google.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Contest photos are public

If you enter the contest, your cat's photo and name may appear on our website and in the calendar sold for your batch.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in those emails. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, contact us. We may need to keep order records for tax and accounting purposes.

Children

Whiskr is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Contest entrants must be 18 or older.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
