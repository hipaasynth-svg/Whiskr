The studio

An original painting of your pet

Not a print. A one-of-a-kind acrylic on canvas, painted by hand by Cody Carlson from a photo you send. It takes two to four weeks because that is how long it takes to paint something properly.

Recent work

Photos of finished commissions are going up here as they come off the bench. In the meantime, codycarlson.art has Cody's wider portfolio.

How it works

  1. 1

    Photo

    You send the reference photo. Good light, eyes visible, taken close up.

  2. 2

    Drawing

    Cody blocks in the drawing and composition on canvas.

  3. 3

    Paint

    Acrylic, built up in layers. This is the part that takes the time.

  4. 4

    Varnish

    Sealed, cured, packed and shipped to you.
