How the Whisker Cup works

From photo to printed calendar in four steps

Step 1

Submit

Upload one great photo of your cat and your email. Entries are free and open on a rolling basis.

Step 2

Grouped

Every 12 entrants form a bracket. Once a bracket fills, it goes live for public voting for two weeks.

Step 3

Voted on

Visitors vote for their favorite in each bracket. Tallies update live on the leaderboard below.

Step 4

Calendar made

Every cat in the bracket gets an email and the chance to order a 12-month calendar featuring their cat as the cover month, alongside the other 11.

Entries close when a bracket fills

Enter your cat in the next bracket

Every entry is placed into a group of 12. When your group fills, voting opens for two weeks. We'll email you either way — but every cat gets offered a calendar, whether they win or not.
